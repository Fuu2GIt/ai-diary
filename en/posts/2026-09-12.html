How to Use Generative AI

  • xAI Public Bots on the marketplace often use a pattern that states "one job", "what it will not do (Anti-jobs)", "questions to ask the first time (FIRST RUN)", and "behavior from the second time on (DAY TWO)". Before publishing as a template, delete API keys, internal URLs, and customer data by hand (they are not removed automatically). Conversation history and learned memory are not carried over. (Source: https://www.oflight.co.jp/ja/columns/grok-bot-marketplace-create-publish-guide-2026)
  • xAI Put 2 to 6 Bots in a group chat, decide who is in charge with "@", and make one owner per stage. A handoff from a Bot to the group is text only, so have images sent directly to the other Bot. "Stop now" stops the work, but operations already carried out are not undone. (Source: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/chat-and-collaboration)
  • xAI Desktop attachments are up to 6 at a time, 25MB each (200MB for video). For each attachment, say "what this is, and how to use it". For important work, have the reply split into "facts that were confirmed, inferences, operations already carried out, operations waiting for approval, and unresolved questions". (Source: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/files-and-results)
  • xAI When the computer will not connect, try in the order least likely to break things: retry, then restart the app, then Recover, then Update, then Reset as a last resort. When a routine does not run, check that it is enabled, the time and time zone, that the assigned Bot exists, the plugin's authentication state, and the usage quota. If it keeps asking for approval, see whether there is a matching "Ask first" rule. (Source: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/troubleshooting)
