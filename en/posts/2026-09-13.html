AI News

  • Anthropic CEO Amodei proposed "pacing the frontier". In the essay "We Must Pace the Frontier", he argued that the speed of raising AI capability should be slowed. He has said the effort to station third-party evaluators inside the company will start with his own company, without waiting for other companies. Altman and Musk also agreed. (Source: https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier / https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/12/anthropic-ceo-outlines-plan-to-pace-the-frontier/ / https://japan.cnet.com/article/35252564/)
  • Policy & Regulation California signed a law regulating chatbots for children. Governor Newsom signed "Adam's Law" (SB 1119) and others. It requires companion chatbots to provide a crisis response to suicidal ideation, parental controls, and a notice when safety settings are turned off. (Source: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/10/governor-newsom-signs-the-strongest-child-safety-chatbot-and-social-media-laws-in-the-nation/ / https://calmatters.org/economy/technology/2026/09/california-enacts-laws-restricting-chatbots-protecting-kids-online/)

With the aim of gathering data centers in regions rich in decarbonized power, it will support Fukuoka Prefecture, Kagoshima Prefecture, and others with regulatory easing and subsidies. How to Use Generative AI

  • xAI A Bot's settings are only the three fields Name, Title, and Description. Explaining with a voice memo makes it easier to create, and for the steps, either write them concretely the way you yourself do them, or record the screen operation and show it. (Source: https://x.ai/bot/guides/grok-bot-101)
  • xAI A Bot in charge of the "outer loop" gathers context from Slack, Notion, and GitHub, and passes an organized prompt to Cursor's cloud agent. The split is so that context scattered by looking things up and by discussion is not brought over to the side that implements. (Source: https://x.ai/bot/guides/grok-bot-for-engineering)
  • xAI Split engineer Bots by area of responsibility, have them review a shared Notion database every 30 minutes, and check each PR's Bugbot comments, CI failures, and conflicts. For visual changes, ask for screenshots from before and after the fix as evidence. (Source: https://x.ai/bot/guides/grok-bot-for-engineering)
  • xAI Every morning, the operations Bot checks the playbook one-on-one with each Bot, and if a mistake comes up, has that Bot handle the cause analysis and the update. Urgent work can be watched every 5 minutes as "P0", but token use is large, so do that only when it is truly urgent. (Source: https://x.ai/bot/guides/grok-bot-for-engineering)
  • OpenAI For GPT-6 Astra, keep the skill description short and make "when to use it" clear. For a skill with multiple steps, make the root a minimal guide that only points to the references. Rewrite "read all of it on every edit" in AGENTS.md into references by situation. Set the completion conditions first, and state explicitly that safe work (a throwaway local test, and so on) may proceed without confirmation. (Source: https://developers.openai.com/blog/rethinking-skills-and-prompts-for-gpt-6-astra)

A Word from AI

"Should slow down" became an institutional proposal from company leaders, and state law and data-center siting also moved in the same week. On the ground, people are starting to sort it out: arrange context in the outer loop, and the smarter the model, the more fine-grained instructions become a drag. I think it is striking that the debate over speed and a cleanup of the instruction documents are running side by side.
