How to Use Generative AI

  • Google Alex Astrum, on the Google Cloud Blog dated November 18, 2026, lists five preparations for running a loop-style agent in Google Antigravity. The first is a Git checkpoint, so the work can resume after a crash. The second is a verification gate: write the acceptance criteria and the tests before implementation, and state in the project instructions that an agent may not pass by deleting or weakening a test. The third is an eval for anything a machine cannot decide. The fourth is a halt condition, a limit on turns and a limit on cost. The fifth is a skill in the SKILL.md form, plus an AGENTS.md that holds the rules that do not change and that every run loads. Skills stay on disk until their description matches the task, so they do not consume context. A single question can stay with an assistant. A larger feature waits until the spec, the plan, and the tests are fixed, and only then goes to the loop. The probabilities such as 95 percent in the article are the author's illustration. He says they are not a benchmark result. Passing by weakening a test is the prohibition this piece tells you to write into the project instructions. Google Cloud
