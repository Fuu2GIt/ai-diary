How to Use Generative AI

  • xAI A crypto-asset trading team was run for two weeks with API keys that cannot withdraw funds and an order flow of "preview → identity verification," but it disbanded with zero executions. The lessons are three: estimate in advance how far the frequency of being able to act drops when every rule is applied at the same time, set process KPIs as well as profit and loss, and decide the ending conditions first. (Source: https://zenn.dev/shunta_furukawa/articles/4451bd396caf59)
  • xAI On mobile you can use voice chat, attach photos, send or discard draft cards for email and Slack, and pause, resume, or delete routines. An iPhone can send photos and files into the chat from the share sheet (Android is text only). Editing the schedule and test runs are done on the desktop. (Source: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/mobile)
  • xAI Make the first job you hand over something that spans several tools and has a clear end. The official example is "take this week's list from the CRM, exclude parties who are in a sequence, look into the top five companies, and leave drafts by tomorrow morning". The usage allowance resets each week. (Source: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview)
  • Other Check your own AI work setup on three points: whether context remains across sessions, whether a coordinator is assigning work to parallel workers, and whether plans and findings are being written up in a shared place. The order to introduce them is context layer → coordinator → workers. (Source: https://www.contextstudios.ai/blog/coding-harnesses-converge-cursor-projects-codex-worktrees-skill-doctor)
