Models that run at hand, AI backing science, and designing how to hand off work

AI News

  • OpenAI Launched the "Rosalind Academic Plan" for life sciences at US university labs (Oct 15). It offers eligible US university labs free access to its life sciences model GPT-Rosalind and Workbench, up to 30 seats for up to 12 months. Use is subject to a trusted-access review. It says research data is not used for training by default, and the API allowance is not included. (Source: https://openai.com/index/introducing-the-rosalind-academic-plan/)
  • OpenAI Anthropic Starting a $15 million total effort supporting math AI together with the Simons Foundation. On Oct 15, the Simons Foundation announced an initiative, with Anthropic and OpenAI together committing $15 million in funding and resources, to help mathematicians verify AI results and work out tool use and norms. The foundation manages the allocations, including open calls. In an update the same day, OpenAI said it will contribute $5 million and provide models and compute. (Source: https://www.simonsfoundation.org/news/simons-foundation-launches-15m-effort-to-help-mathematicians-navigate-ai-era/ / https://openai.com/index/sharing-ai-progress-in-mathematics/)
  • Anthropic It reportedly told pre-IPO investors that Q3 2026 revenue was $16.8 billion and that it was profitable on an adjusted basis. CNBC and the FT reported, citing people familiar, that in a briefing for investors as it prepares for an IPO, Anthropic said Q3 revenue was $16.8 billion (up from about $11.6 billion in Q2 and about $4.7 billion in Q1), profitable on an adjusted basis, with positive cash flow. There are also reports that its debut could come around November. This is not an official, finalized earnings announcement from the company. (Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/15/anthropics-third-quarter-revenue-hits-16point8-billion-ipo.html / https://www.ft.com/content/11632d03-358c-454b-af95-d06d4072932b)
  • NVIDIA Released "Nemotron 3.5" open models for agents, Super VL and Bolt (Oct 15). It announced "Nemotron 3.5 Super VL" (around 120 billion parameters) for always-on agents, and "Nemotron 3.5 Bolt" (74B-A9B, 24GB, and others) for local use. Weights, datasets, and recipes are open, and they are available on Hugging Face, NVIDIA NIM, and elsewhere. Bolt E4B is scheduled for Oct 23. (Source: https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nemotron-3-5-super-vl/)
  • Google Published a workflow for finding antimicrobial peptide candidates with Antigravity (Oct 15). Google DeepMind published a technical walkthrough of using the agentic science workbench "Google Antigravity" to narrow down, from unannotated sequences in UniProt, bacteriocin candidates (for example A0A0U0D591) predicted to be active against respiratory pathogens. Wet-lab validation is ongoing, and a definitive drug effect has not been confirmed. (Source: https://deepmind.google/blog/accelerating-antimicrobial-discovery/)
  • Meta Released the open-weight "Muse Spark Open." US-based Meta released it on Oct 15 (US time). It has 816 billion total parameters and 4.2 billion active parameters, a 1-million-token context, and is natively multimodal, aimed at coding and agent work. Weights are distributed through Hugging Face and AWS / Google Cloud / Microsoft, and it says the model can also run locally on two Mac Studios with Apple M5 Ultra (about 1 TB of unified memory in total). It is an open version on a separate line from the top-end "Muse Wonder" announced on Oct 12. (Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/15/meta-launches-an-open-weight-model-that-runs-on-macs/ / https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/8eb2affea67967c392d778067d4dac05ab7b87eb)

How to Use Generative AI

  • Anthropic Give Claude Code a verification signal. Always attach to an implementation request a pass/fail check Claude can run itself, such as tests, a build, or a screenshot comparison. Go in the order explore → plan → implement, and keep CLAUDE.md short, with only the "lines that would cause mistakes if removed." Use /clear between unrelated tasks. Put occasionally used procedures in Skills rather than in always-on rules. (Source: https://code.claude.com/docs/en/best-practices)
  • Anthropic Unattended agents on Opus 5.5 should not treat end_turn as completion. Re-measure effort starting from medium (don't carry over Opus 5's high). Treat a text-only end_turn as a progress report, and if the checklist has unfinished items, send a short "continue" message. Stop automatic continuation on the same task after 2 to 3 times. Optionally, add to the system prompt "write the status in the same message as the next tool call and keep going," so it doesn't stop at a mere check-in. (Source: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-opus-5-5)
  • Cursor Turn it into a skill first, then a routine. Settle the procedure in a chat once, save it as a Skill, and automate it with a Routine once it reproduces. Use "Teach a task" to show a browser operation once and create a draft Skill, then Test it with safe input before scheduling. Put sending, purchasing, deleting, and publishing behind approval, and on days when the source is missing or stale, report a failure instead of running on old data. (Source: https://cursor.com/docs/grok-bot/work.md)
  • Other First-time setup for the public template "Gmail morning triage." After adding the template, check the skills/routines it contains, choose A (hand materials to the main bot) or B (report only), answer the time and the criteria for "needs action," and reconnect your own Gmail. The template is only a recipe and does not carry the author's login or history. You can also set it not to notify on days with nothing that needs action. (Source: https://tomyunser.com/grok-bot-template-gmail-morning/)
