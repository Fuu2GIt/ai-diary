How to Use Generative AI

  • xAI Takuya Kawai on NewsPicks, dated November 10, 2026, writes six tips for handing work to an AI, based on advice from Lauren Tan, whom the article describes as a former Cursor engineer involved in building Grok Bot. The six are: teach the background, check the understanding, connect the information, keep the procedure, grow from a few, and ask for help designing the Bot. He says to hand over the purpose, the usual work, and the finished form, not only a job title. Asking, before the work starts, "explain this request back in your own words" is how he says a mismatch in purpose or finished form shows up early. The article states that the sample requests are his own practical drafts built from Tan's advice, not a word-for-word translation and not a report of results he measured on Grok Bot. A note at the end says the product details were checked on November 5, 2026, and that the published requests were not tested on a live Grok Bot. The article puts that limit in two places: the requests are his drafts, and they are not a record of a Grok Bot run. NewsPicks
  • Other grokguide.jp, updated November 16, 2026 and operated by 株式会社ZETTAI, says it is an independent guide. A first request, it says, should include five things: the purpose, the source, the scope, the deliverable, and the approval. Purpose is what should be finished. Source is the URL or file to read. Scope is the period, the data that may be touched, and the actions not to take. The deliverable is the format and where to save it. Approval means sending, changing, and deleting wait for the person.
