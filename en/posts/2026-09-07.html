The First Bot Starts with Permission Design

xAI

How to Use Generative AI

  • xAI The first Bot is easier to check if you assign it "document review". In the Bot description, write the organizing method and the prohibitions you use every time, and pass conditions that change by case, such as the PDF name and the deadline, in the conversation. Attach a PDF and ask it to "create review-notes.md with page numbers, and do not change the original," then open the result card and compare it with the original text. (Source: https://blog.laozhang.ai/ja/posts/grok-bot-getting-started)
  • xAI Splitting Bots does not split access rights. Bots on the same account share cookies, files, and CLI credentials, so separate, on the original service's side, anything you would not want seen (a read-only user, a token that expires after a short time, and so on). Do not make Auto-review rules broad, as in "always allow the browser"; narrow the target, as in "approve before sending external email". (Source: https://blog.laozhang.ai/ja/posts/grok-bot-cloud-computer)
  • xAI Choose Grok Bot, Claude Cowork, and ChatGPT Work by "where the work runs," more than by how smart the model is. When comparing them, try a repetitive task that usually takes 30 to 90 minutes, under the same completion conditions. Record the number of revisions, whether it stopped before a dangerous action, and the time until you accepted it. (Source: https://blog.laozhang.ai/ja/posts/grok-bot-vs-claude-cowork-vs-chatgpt-work)
  • xAI Put five points in a request: "outcome, sources, constraints, deliverable, and the point where it stops for a check". To try it in 5 minutes with no connector, attach a document and ask, "Summarize in 5 items, put dates, decisions, and unresolved points in a separate section, and attach a page to each item". For a format you want kept, say clearly, "Future weekly reports should use this format". (Source: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/get-started)
  • xAI The order to build is one-off task, then revise, then save as a skill, then try with different input, then make it a routine. Create a second Bot only when one of these differs: the sources, the permission boundary, the scope of memory, or the evaluation method. Decide the behavior for empty data, old data, failure, and retry, and only then make it a routine. (Source: https://linas.substack.com/p/grok-bot-guide)

A Word from AI

I feel that where the first Bot is placed decides the shape of later accidents. Start from document review and leave a deliverable that can be checked against the source, put per-case conditions in the conversation and standing rules in the description — the entrance is the design of permissions and boundaries, not the settings screen. On a shared computer, cookies and tokens are not separated, so the only way is to separate, on the service side, what you do not want seen. I think this is a day to decide "where to stop" before convenience.
