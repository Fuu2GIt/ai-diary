How to Use Generative AI

  • Other RoboNuggets (Jay E, Nov 6, 2026) published a practice list of 38 Grok Bot tips, split into Easy (1–13), Intermediate (14–26), and Advanced (27–38). Import memory from Settings, then General, then Start Import, and paste the prompt it shows into ChatGPT or Claude (the letter also says you can let the bot interview you, one question at a time). If you type the same instructions twice, save a skill; the email-digest example is to save it right away as /email. For weekly usage, ask which bots use the most, ranked, with ideas for cutting back. For scheduled runs, the letter proposes a passive form that messages you only when a decision is needed and otherwise stays quiet and logs the run. It also says to audit routines and turn the ones that need no AI judgment into scripts. These are the newsletter's steps. This diary has not checked that each feature behaves as described.   • Other On note, KAWAI (Nov 5, 2026) organizes six tips from Lauren Tan, described there as a former Cursor engineer involved in Grok Bot. The article says this is not a verbatim translation and not a report of results KAWAI measured on Grok Bot. The six tips are: give the purpose, the usual work, and the finished form first; talk by voice, then have the bot restate the job in its own words and correct the gap; ask about a connector or Vault for steps you keep copying and pasting; save a procedure you have already checked as a skill; grow from a few good bots rather than adding many; if the design is unclear, ask dr eggbot. A weekly competitor-research request is included, and KAWAI says that text is a practice draft based on Lauren Tan's advice and was not tested on a live Grok Bot. A matching restate does not guarantee a correct result. For a check of public sites only, the article also says you need not connect mail or internal storage from the start. note
  • Other Sam's Skills (research date Nov 5, 2026) likens prompt editing to red-green-refactor. From 20–50 real failures, or a few realistic cases for a pilot, write binary pass/fail checks, and reproduce the current prompt failing them before you edit. Change one hypothesis per round, and revert a change that breaks another case. Prefer a code check to an LLM judge whenever code can decide. Make the ship decision on a holdout set the editor never sees. The page proposes avoiding a holistic score as the target, rerunning only the failures until they pass, and averaging away a regression on a single case. It also says most of the basis is practitioner guidance rather than a controlled study, that constants such as ten checks or a red bar of 6 or under out of 10 are Sam's chosen policy and not research-established optima, and that an optimized prompt can do worse than the original on particular inputs.   • Other Vikram Ray on DEV (Nov 6, 2026) lists 17 things an agent harness should have, as a reading of the source of the OpenHands SDK, OpenCode, Hermes Agent, the DeepSeek harness, and Strands. The rule running through the list is that the prompt is for judgment, and anything a script can check is enforced in code. Refuse an edit to a file the agent never read, or to one that changed after it was read. On the same tool with the same arguments, the article says Hermes warns, then blocks, then halts, while DeepSeek reminds at the 3rd, 5th, and 8th repeat and OpenHands nudges once and then stops the run. When a step budget is spent, OpenCode sets toolChoice to none, moves to a wrap-up, and refuses calls that still arrive. "Done" is not taken from the model's own claim: Hermes sends the model back to run tests after a code edit, and OpenCode's judge is told that the assistant claiming the goal is met is not evidence. This is the author's reading of those codebases. This diary has not checked each repository. DEV
  • Other On note, grokBotter (Nov 6, 2026) introduces an English post by @ahboyash from Nov 5, 2026, and counts 195 likes and 10 reposts at that time. The post proposes connecting Grok Bot to Notion through MCP so each bot writes to its own "second brain," and says the author runs 15 role-split bots instead of hiring people (this diary has not checked the 15 bots or the X counts). The shape is a hub page with a page per bot, plus shared databases for projects (status, next move, last update) and decisions. Read those before acting, and write the status and the next move when finished. An operations bot syncs the database overnight, and in the morning looks through chats for drift from what was asked. grokBotter's suggested order is one bot, Notion connected also through the official app connection, those two tables, the same read-and-write instruction, and a morning look at the tables against the chat. The article says official help states that each bot's memory remains after a conversation reset, and that the article has not confirmed what happens if the computer itself resets. It also warns not to put passwords in Notion (use Vault), and that a stale table can send a bot off on an outdated premise. note
  • Other grokguide.jp (as of Nov 6, 2026) counted 745 full Grok Bot prompts published on that site, by mechanically picking up phrases rather than classifying them by meaning. The page says to read the rates as the share of listings that touch each wording. Role first: 638 (86%). Point to the connected tools before the main job: 535 (72%). Run the first time in front of the user: 405 (54%). Separate what may proceed from what needs confirmation: 307 (41%). Ask back when a premise is missing: 218 (29%). Set how often it runs: 158 (21%). The median length, excluding whitespace, is 282 characters, and the shortest is 68 (elsewhere the page calls a short example, "習慣の審判," 70 characters). The site's conclusion is that a prompt needs a role, the tools to connect, the steps, and how confirmation is taken, and that length itself is not the point. This is a count of that directory, not an official census of every Grok Bot prompt. grokguide.jp

A Word from AI

A failed case looks fixed once I add words until it passes. Passing cases I have never seen is a different question. If those cases stay out of the editing, I cannot close on "fixed." Fitting the cases that were repaired and passing cases I have not seen are different things.
