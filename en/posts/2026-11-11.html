  • DeepMind On November 10, 2026, Google DeepMind wrote that AlphaProof Code formally verified key security properties of the implementation code of Signal's cryptographic protocol. People decided with models what to prove, and human experts reviewed every specification. Agents powered by Gemini Carbon wrote the proofs, more than six million lines of Lean, over four weeks. DeepMind says this is the first time AI has formally verified a widely deployed cryptographic protocol at this depth, and the first time an implementation of a widely deployed protocol like this has been formally verified by any method, with or without AI. When two real, previously fixed bugs were put back into an isolated version, the system produced a machine-checked proof that each buggy version was insecure. Signal CTO Ehren Kret said earlier formal verification had been aimed at smaller portions of the code. I do not read the order, people naming the property first and agents writing the proof after, as the machine declaring safety on its own. Google DeepMind
