How to Use Generative AI

  • xAI @AkioT315 wrote in an X post on November 27 that Grok Bot can now read Obsidian notes, but that installing the plugin does not turn them into memory, and said the article lays out how to install the official plugin and how to start with requests that do not write anything. An article with the same title says that to read the notes in a vault you add the official plugin "Obsidian for Grok Bot," and that the route that operates the local Obsidian app requires updating Obsidian to 1.12.7 or later and turning on the command line interface. It advises starting with a vault that holds nothing confidential and with a request that does not write, such as listing the notes, comparing the result with Obsidian itself, and only then moving on to low-impact writes. It also describes a split: keep the information you rely on in your own notes, and use Grok Bot's memory for preferences and working rules. Obsidian's official help likewise says to update to 1.12.7 or later and to turn on the command line interface under Settings → General. @AkioT315 Obsidian ヘルプ
  • xAI The AI Productivity Insights tutorial writes that Grok Bot's changelog entry v1.2.3, dated November 18, 2026 and titled "Tool permissions and routine reconnect cards," lets you set each tool on a connector page to Always allow, Auto-review, Needs approval or Blocked, and that the feature requires Grok Bot 0.70.2 or later. It recommends first listing every tool on the connector and sorting it into one of four groups: only reads or searches; creates, sends or publishes something; deletes or changes something that is hard to undo; and not needed by your workflow. It then suggests Auto-review for reads and searches, Needs approval for creating, sending or publishing, Needs approval for deleting or hard-to-undo changes (or Blocked if the workflow never deletes), and Blocked for tools that are not needed, and says this pairing is its own suggestion, not an official definition. In its Canva example, all 44 tools sat in a single group called Writes, including list-folder-items and search-folders, whose names suggest they only read, so it says not to trust the group label alone and to sort by what each tool does.
