  • DeepMind On November 11, 2026, Google DeepMind announced AlphaGenome 2. It predicts how DNA variants affect specific human cell types. The blog says the model was trained on more than 100 million individual cell profiles across 68 tissues, and a later paragraph says over 107 million. It identifies nearly twice as many variants that change gene expression as the lab's previous state-of-the-art model. It can predict unseen single-cell experiments and cell states without retraining. Non-commercial research can use it from today through the AlphaGenome API, and commercial research is in a limited private preview. A note says it has not been validated or approved for clinical or diagnostic use. The Verge reported the same announcement the same day. Google DeepMindThe Verge
