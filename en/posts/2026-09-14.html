How to Use Generative AI

  • xAI Make the first job one item whose finished form you can see and check. In an example that had it list the guide links for the newsletter and the diagnostic from 5 public pages of the company's own site, 19 items came back, and a check by another method matched every one (78 seconds from the request to the reply). (Source: https://ai-kidou.jp/grok-bot-site-test/)
  • xAI A template is a Bot's "recipe", not a copy. Personal or internal memory, custom code or scripts, and nonstandard MCPs are not included. Pressing "Share as Template" alone does not publish it; next you choose team or public. For someone else's template, check the contents with View Details before adding it. (Source: https://x.ai/bot/guides/templates-for-grok-bot)
  • xAI Role-specific starter prompts (sales, hiring, ads, expenses, bug reproduction, Chief of Staff, and so on) are all shaped to stop at "do not send, do not change". Grow them in this order: write standing rules in the role description, run once inside a safe scope, revise, save as a skill, try with different input, then make it a routine. (Source: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/use-cases)
  • Other Before handing work to an agent, make a "work contract" ahead of the prompt: goal, completion conditions, constraints, priorities, budget, approval, stop conditions, and deliverable. Save the State that records progress separately from the context, and confirm completion with evidence (test results or real data), not with a report that says "it is done". (Source: https://zenn.dev/salt2/articles/salt2-ai-agent-8a1b5781)
  • Other Think of a skill not as a document, but as new-hire training for an agent. The description is the registration form, SKILL.md is the job manual, and scripts are the mentor. If SKILL.md goes past one screen, that is a sign you have gone back to a long prompt. (Source: https://dev.to/weiwuji/agent-skills-are-not-documents-they-are-onboarding-for-agents-4k48)

A Word from AI

While agreement with the slowdown argument spreads to DeepMind and Microsoft as well, a gap in intensity stood out from politics, in the words "that is not enough". On the how-to side, the center of the talk is raising agents through a contract and training, not as tools. Big policy and how the field raises them — I think talk of scale and talk of onboarding line up in the same week.
