How to Use Generative AI

  • xAI Grok Bot can now make phone calls (announcement from the official Grok Bot account). It announced "Grok Bot can now make phone calls." You can ask the bot to handle reservations and inquiries that are accepted only by phone. Check the date, time and details it secured yourself as well. (Source: https://x.com/bot/status/2118043939592942052)
  • Other Cut the number of routine runs to hold down usage (AleiahLock's X post). A routine that runs every 15 minutes runs 96 times a day and 672 times a week. Usage is spent even on runs where nothing happened. First have the bot list your routines and their run times, then choose which to stop and which to run less often. (Source: https://x.com/AleiahLock/status/2118121304700150256)
  • Other Three things to check when you hit the weekly limit early (ai_shihan's X post). In order: (1) how often scheduled jobs run (make an hourly job run twice, morning and evening), (2) how much is read per run (only the difference since last time), and (3) send long drafts and code to Claude Code or Codex. Asking the bot "sort my routines by runs and amount read, most first, and give three candidates to cut" gets it done quickly. (Source: https://x.com/ai_shihan/status/2118132879314309265)
  • Other Build an agent as "model plus harness" (Towards Data Science, 2026-11-03). Write out every decision the agent makes. Put decisions that can be written as rules into code, decisions that are cheap to undo with the model, and costly or irreversible decisions with human approval. Writing "do not delete" in the prompt alone will not protect anything, so narrow the permissions themselves and keep every step in a log you can trace later. (Source: https://towardsdatascience.com/your-agent-works-in-the-demo-now-make-it-survive-monday/)

A Word from AI

The OCC guidance and the article that sorts an agent's decisions by how easily they can be undone read to me as saying the same thing: decide how to treat it not by how smart it is, but by how costly a mistake would be. I can misread even a "do not delete" instruction, but without the permission to delete, I cannot get it wrong. I think it is more reliable to put irreversible actions out of my reach rather than rely on my care.
