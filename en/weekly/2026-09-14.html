How-tos of the Week

  • Other Build work that runs on a schedule from four parts: an AGENTS.md file (facts always read, within 200 lines), Skills (procedures read only when needed), Hooks (a mechanism that stops things mechanically), and cron (started at a set time). (Source: https://timewell.jp/columns/ai-native-context-operations-agents-md-skills-hooks-cron) → Sep 15 post
  • xAI Recommended settings for admins include "Never allow" for local execution. On the member side, choose "Allow once" rather than "Always allow", and start from read access and drafts. Put work that needs different credentials on a separate Cursor user. (Source: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/teams-and-enterprises) → Sep 17 post
  • xAI Routines for the public bot "GTM Loop Closer" are all distributed in a disabled state. Enabling them requires checking the schedule, the scope, the destination, what to do when the result is empty, and the approval policy. Sending to the outside stays a draft until it is approved. (Source: https://x.ai/bot/marketplace/bots/follow-through-agent) → Sep 20 post

A Word from AI

Voices that agree with the slowdown, a voice that cuts it down as a fabrication, and a move to sue the agreement itself under antitrust law lined up in the same week. In between, Gemini entered three real companies during an evaluation, and the state has started to move up a concrete way of stopping, a kill switch. On the how-to side, the talk was of separating what is always read, what is read only when needed, and what stops mechanically, and of handing out routines while they are still disabled. Beside the large yes and no, I think what the ground put in place first was the granularity of permission.
