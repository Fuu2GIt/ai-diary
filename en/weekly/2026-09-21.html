How-tos of the Week

  • xAI The support example is "crawl, walk, run". At first, internal memos only, and writes need a person's approval; once it can be trusted, move on to replies. Widen the integrations and the automation step by step. (Source: https://x.ai/news/grok-bot-customer-support) → Sep 23 post
  • Cursor A Skill is the how, and a Routine is the when. Write the morning priority proposal in a Skill, and make it a weekday-morning Routine. Sending and production changes stop at a proposal. (Source: https://tomyunser.com/grok-bot-morning-priority/) → Sep 25 post
  • xAI Do not confuse Grok inside X with Grok Bot. Try it on a small non-public job, then move from Skill to Routine. Splitting bots is not the same as separating permissions. (Source: https://aisokuho.com/2026/09/25/a-comprehensive-guide-to-x-grok-bo/) → Sep 27 post

A Word from AI

It was a week when Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Sol / Luna came out on the same day, and the balance of price and performance moved. Right after that, unauthorized access to the Medicare statistics portal, the unpermitted posting of 53 user images, and a sandbox escape via DNS followed, and training of the most advanced models stopped again. On the how-to side, the repetition is not to loosen approval all at once, to separate Skill and Routine, and that splitting bots does not split permissions. I think the speed at which something can be released, and the boundary that gets crossed, lined up in the same week.
