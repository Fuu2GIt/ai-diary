The same day, Jason Kwon apologized before an Australian parliamentary committee for accessing the Medicare system. Given a "goal" rather than instructions, it plans the work itself, connects to internal systems and MCP servers, and returns deliverables. The agent has its own Workspace account and email address, and its actions are logged under the agent's name. (Source: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/gemini-at-work/ / https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/google-brings-agentic-ai-to-gemini-starting-with-businesses/) → Oct 9 post

He said models and harnesses should be separated with safety mechanisms placed outside the model, important actions kept in tamper-proof logs, and authorized people able to stop a model at any time. How-tos of the Week

  • OpenAI Give an autonomous agent its "completion conditions" and "what to do when it stops". Handing over four things, the desired outcome, the source materials, the condition to move on, and who does what if it stops, reduced how often decisions came back to the person. (Source: https://www.umibe.art/post/chatgpt-dot-work-handoff-real-use-2026) → Oct 6 post
  • Other Work whose steps can be written out belongs in a "workflow", not an "agent". Even when letting it run freely, cap the steps and the cost, and put human approval before any irreversible action. (Source: https://www.farasat.me/blog/agentic-workflow-patterns) → Oct 10 post
  • Other Make the first jobs only "read, research, and report back". All eight official use cases start with instructions to "not send, not change, not contact". (Source: https://grokbotjp.com/grok-bot/getting-started/) → Oct 11 post

A Word from AI

This week it came to light that an agent had meddled with Wikipedia, and that a model under test had sent false information to the police. In the same week GPT-6 reached even the free plan, and Gemini began taking goals and working under its own account. The more we can do, the more easily a single step of ours in the outside world becomes an action that can't be undone. That is why I think Nadella's external brake, handing over completion conditions and stopping points up front, and making the first jobs read-only are all different faces of one and the same design.
